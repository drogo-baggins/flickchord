FAQ

The sound is late with wireless headphones
Bluetooth headphones and speakers delay the sound by 0.1–0.3 s, and AirPlay by about 2 s. This comes from the connection itself and the app can't shorten it. Use the iPhone speaker or wired headphones.
There's no sound
Only the plate under your right hand makes sound; pressing a pad alone is silent. Check your iPhone volume and where the sound is going (headphones or a speaker). The app plays even when the Ring/Silent switch or Silent mode is on.
Opening the app stops music from other apps
To play reliably as an instrument, the app doesn't mix with other apps' audio.
Can I use it on iPad?
It runs on iPad in iPhone mode, but it's designed to be held and played on an iPhone.
Where is my play diary saved?
Only on your iPhone. It's never sent to the developer. It is included in your iPhone backups, including iCloud. You can delete diary entries in the app.
I'd like a refund
Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
