Privacy Policy

The app

  • The app does not collect any personal information or data from anyone, including children.
  • The app makes no network connections. It has no ads, analytics or tracking.
  • App data (settings, guide progress, your play diary and notes) is stored only on your iPhone and is never sent to the developer. Depending on your iPhone settings, it may be included in iCloud or device backups, and copies stay in those backups after you delete the app. You can also delete diary entries in the app.
  • If you turn on "Share with App Developers" in your iPhone settings, Apple may share crash reports and similar information with the developer. This is controlled by your iPhone settings and Apple's privacy policy.
  • Tapping the link on the Credits screen opens an external website (GitHub) in your web browser. That site's own privacy policy applies there.

Emails you send us

  • We use your name, email address and message only to reply, and delete them when no longer needed. We never share them.
  • If we learn that a child under 13 has sent us personal information, we will delete it.
  • The developer's name and contact details are available on request.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security (see the GitHub General Privacy Statement). The developer uses no analytics or cookies.

The home page embeds a YouTube video, using the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com. When you play it, YouTube (Google) may receive information, and the Google Privacy Policy applies.

If this policy changes, we will post the update on this page.
